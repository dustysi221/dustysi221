04 VOICE ENGINEER (PUSH TO TALK)
================================
Ask the engineer a question out loud and hear the answer in your headset.

  You:       "How are my tires looking?"
  Engineer:  "Copy that. Front left running hot, take 0.2 PSI out at the
              stop. Box end of lap 30."

Speech to text and text to speech are FREE (built into Chrome/Edge).
Only the answer is an AI call: about 1 cent per question.

NEEDS: the AI turned on (see 03), Chrome or Edge (NOT Firefox), a microphone,
headset/speakers, and an internet connection.

-----------------------------------------------------------------
1. EVERY TIME YOU OPEN THE DASHBOARD
-----------------------------------------------------------------
Click "Enable voice" (top right). The button changes to "Push to talk".
The first time, the browser asks for the microphone: click Allow.

-----------------------------------------------------------------
2. ONE-TIME SETUP (settings are remembered by the browser)
-----------------------------------------------------------------
a) Press any button on your wheel (so the browser detects it).
b) Click the gear icon (top right) -> "Voice - push to talk".
   "Detected" should show your wheel's name.
c) Click "Assign" and press the wheel button you want to use.
   The line shows "Wheel button: <your wheel> - button <number>".
d) Pick an "Engineer voice" (Edge's "Natural" voices sound best).
e) "Speech speed" if it talks too fast or slow.
f) Click "Test voice" -> you should hear "Radio check...".
g) Optional: tick "Read out new engineer calls" to also hear the tire
   engineer and strategist whenever their advice changes.

-----------------------------------------------------------------
3. HOW TO TALK
-----------------------------------------------------------------
1. HOLD your wheel button.
   (Or hold V on the keyboard with the dashboard clicked, or hold the mic
   button with the mouse.)
2. A red bar says "Listening..." and shows your words as you speak.
3. RELEASE when you're done talking.
4. The bar shows what it heard, then the answer. You hear the answer, and
   it's added to the Engineer radio feed (YOU -> ENG).
5. Pressing the button while the engineer is talking cuts them off.

Follow-ups work: it remembers your last few questions.
  "How are my tires?"  ->  "What about the rears?"

-----------------------------------------------------------------
4. GOOD THINGS TO ASK
-----------------------------------------------------------------
  "How are my tires looking?"
  "When should I pit?"            (after 2-3 laps, it needs fuel data)
  "How much fuel am I using?"
  "Can I make it to the end on this fuel?"
  "What's the gap to the car ahead?"
  "Should I push or save the tires?"
  "What pressure changes for the next stop?"

-----------------------------------------------------------------
5. IMPORTANT
-----------------------------------------------------------------
- Keep the dashboard window VISIBLE on your second monitor (not minimized),
  or the browser stops reading the wheel.
- Whether the wheel button works while LMU has focus depends on the browser.
  Test it (see 05, or TESTING.md stage 3b). If it doesn't work in Chrome,
  try Edge.
- Your recording is sent to Google (Chrome) or Microsoft (Edge) to turn it
  into text. It's free but needs internet.
- The engineer's personality, answer length and radio phrases are in
  src\voice-prompts.js. Edit that file and restart to change them.

-----------------------------------------------------------------
6. VOICE PROBLEMS
-----------------------------------------------------------------
"Voice unsupported"        -> use Chrome or Edge, not Firefox.
"Voice is off"             -> click "Enable voice" first.
"Microphone blocked"       -> click the icon left of localhost:3000 in the
                              address bar -> allow Microphone -> reload.
                              Also Windows Settings -> Privacy & security ->
                              Microphone -> on for desktop apps.
Red bar but no words       -> wrong mic: chrome://settings/content/microphone
                              and pick your headset mic.
"Didn't catch that"        -> hold the whole time; start talking after the
                              red bar appears.
"Claude is off"            -> no API key (see 03).
Words appear, no sound     -> gear icon -> Test voice; check Windows sound
                              output is your headset.
Wheel not "Detected"       -> press a wheel button with the dashboard
                              visible, then reopen the gear menu.
