07 COSTS AND MONEY
==================
The AI is REAL MONEY. Every AI call is charged to your Anthropic account.

HOW YOU PAY
-----------
- You add credit at https://console.anthropic.com (Billing).
- Calls are taken from that balance. When it runs out, the AI stops (the
  dashboard itself keeps working).
- A Claude.ai subscription (Pro/Max) does NOT cover it. The API is separate.

WHAT IS FREE
------------
- The dashboard, telemetry, simulator.
- Voice: speech-to-text and text-to-speech (built into the browser).

WHAT COSTS MONEY (estimates, per hour of driving)
-------------------------------------------------
  Opus 5, tire check every 5 s (default)   about $23 / hour
  Opus 5, every 15 s                       about $8 / hour
  Sonnet 5, every 5 s                      about $9 / hour
  Sonnet 5, every 10 s                     about $5 / hour
  Haiku 4.5, every 5 s                     about $3.50 / hour
  Voice question                           about 1 cent each (Opus), less with Sonnet
  Strategist                               about $0.50-1.30 / hour
These are estimates (+/- 50%).

The AI only runs while the dashboard is open AND you're on track (not
paused, not in menus). Close the black window when done = no more cost.

SEE THE REAL COST
-----------------
- Dashboard: the Engineer radio header shows calls and cost this session
  (an estimate).
- Exact charges: https://console.anthropic.com -> Usage.

PROTECT YOURSELF
----------------
- Only add as much credit as you're happy to spend. Keep auto-reload OFF.
- Set a spend limit in the Console.
- Use ANALYSIS_MODEL=claude-sonnet-5 and/or ANALYSIS_INTERVAL_MS=15000.
