LMU TELEMETRY DASHBOARD - NOTES
================================

Your project folder:
  C:\Users\pkim5\dustysi221\lmu-telemetry-dashboard

What it is:
  A dashboard for your second monitor while you drive Le Mans Ultimate.
  It shows your tires, speed, fuel and lap times live, and has an AI race
  engineer (Claude) that:
    - checks your tires every few seconds (Tire engineer)
    - makes pit and fuel calls after every lap (Strategist)
    - answers questions you ask out loud with a wheel button (Voice engineer)

-----------------------------------------------------------------
QUICK START (every time you race)
-----------------------------------------------------------------
1. Double-click  "Start Dashboard.bat"  in the project folder.
   A black window opens (leave it open) and the dashboard opens in your
   browser at http://localhost:3000
   It opens in your DEFAULT browser. If that's Firefox, copy the address
   into Chrome or Edge instead (voice doesn't work in Firefox).
2. Move the browser window to your second monitor.
3. Click "Enable voice" (top right of the dashboard) and allow the microphone.
4. Start LMU and drive out of the garage. The pill at the top right turns
   green and says "Live".
5. Hold your wheel button to talk to the engineer.
6. When you're done, close the black window.

To test without the game: double-click "Start Simulator.bat" instead.
To get the newest version: double-click "Update Dashboard.bat".
