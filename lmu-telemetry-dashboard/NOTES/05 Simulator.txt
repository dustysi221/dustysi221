05 SIMULATOR (TESTING WITHOUT THE GAME)
=======================================
A fake car that drives a 90-minute race with 5 AI rivals and one pit stop
(around lap 30). Everything on the dashboard works with it, including the AI
and voice. The AI still costs money in the simulator.

START IT
--------
Double-click "Start Simulator.bat".
(Manual way: in PowerShell in the project folder, type  npm run mock )

The black window says "Telemetry source: mock". The dashboard pill says
"Live - simulator".

MAKE IT FASTER
--------------
Laps normally take 100 seconds. To make it 10x faster:
  1. Close the black window.
  2. In PowerShell in the project folder:   Add-Content .env "MOCK_SPEED=10"
  3. Start the simulator again. The window says "(x10 speed)".
REMOVE that line again before racing (notepad .env, delete it, Save).
It only affects the simulator, but it's easy to forget.

Faster = more strategy calls per minute = more cost.

WHAT TO CHECK
-------------
- Speed, gear, RPM, tires all moving.
- Fuel per lap about 2.9 L after two laps.
- Strategist mentions a stop around lap 29-30, fuel only.
- Voice: hold V, ask "How much fuel am I using?" -> about 2.9 liters a lap.

STOP IT
-------
Close the black window.
