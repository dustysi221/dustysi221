05 SIMULATOR (TESTING WITHOUT THE GAME)
=======================================
A full simulated session on a 5.4 km circuit with 7 rivals in two classes
(Hypercar and LMGT3): real braking and acceleration, apex speeds, tire
temperatures and wear, fuel, pit lane and garages, flags, safety car.
Everything works with it: driver dashboard, engineer view, AI engineers and
voice. The AI still costs money in the simulator.

It is the same simulator as the online demo and  /engineer?demo , so what
you see there is what the server simulator does.

START IT
--------
Double-click "Start Simulator.bat".
(Manual way: in PowerShell in the project folder, type  npm run mock )

The black window says "Telemetry source: mock". The dashboard pill says
"Live - simulator".

SESSIONS (in the Control Panel, Telemetry source card)
--------
  Practice ...... 60 min. Cars leave the garage in stints: out lap, 4-7
                  timed laps, in lap, time in the garage. Ranked by best lap.
  Qualifying .... 15 min. Out lap, two push laps, cool-down lap, back to the
                  garage. Ranked by best lap.
  Race .......... 90 min. Rolling start: 10 s on the grid, formation lap
                  behind the pace car, green flag at the line. Chequered
                  flag and a cool-down lap at the end.
                  Run Qualifying first and the race grid follows its result.
The server starts in the race. To start in another session, add to .env:
  MOCK_SESSION=practice      (or qualifying)

RACE CONTROL (Control Panel)
------------
  Yellow ....... a car stops, its sector goes yellow
  FCY .......... full course yellow, 80 km/h (race only)
  Safety car ... cars queue behind it at 120 km/h (race only)
  Green ........ ends the current one
They also happen by themselves every 15 minutes in the race.

MAKE IT FASTER
--------------
In the Control Panel: Simulator speed (real time up to 20x). It changes
straight away and the session keeps going.
Faster = more strategy calls per minute = more AI cost.

WHAT TO CHECK
-------------
- Speed, gear, RPM, pedals and tires all moving like a real lap.
- Fuel per lap about 1.6 L after two green laps.
- Engineer view: traces, sectors, laps, flags on the map.
- Voice: hold your button, ask "How much fuel am I using?".

STOP IT
-------
Close the black window.
