ENGINEER VIEW (THE SECOND DASHBOARD)
====================================

There are two dashboards. Both run from the same server at the same time:

  Driver dashboard   http://localhost:3000            big numbers, AI engineers, voice
  Engineer view      http://localhost:3000/engineer   pit-wall telemetry like GT3 / F1 teams

Open the engineer view with the "Engineer view" button at the top of the
driver dashboard or the Control Panel, or type the address in the browser.
It makes no AI calls, so it costs nothing.

-----------------------------------------------------------------
WHAT'S ON IT
-----------------------------------------------------------------
Top bar     Session, time left, lap, position, last / best lap, and the
            LIVE DELTA to your best lap (green = faster, red = slower).

Timing      Every car: position, class color, gap, last lap, best lap.
(left)      "Leader" = gap to P1, "Interval" = gap to the car ahead.
            PIT = in the pits. FL = fastest lap of the session.
            Your row is highlighted. On smaller screens the Last column is
            hidden; hover a car on the track map to see it.

Traces      Speed, throttle, brake, gear, RPM and steering over one lap.
(middle)    Solid line = this lap. Dashed line = your best clean lap.
            The top strip is the delta: going up means losing time there.
            Move the mouse over the traces to compare both laps at that spot.

Sectors     Your sector times. Green = personal best, yellow = slower.
Laps        One row per lap: time, difference to best, sectors, top speed,
            fuel used. The green dot marks your best lap. Grey = out lap,
            pit lap or a lap you joined halfway (not used as a reference).

Right side  Track map (draws itself during your first lap; hover a dot to
            see the car), G-force circle, pedal/steering bars, the four
            tires (inner / middle / outer temps, pressure, wear, core and
            brake temperature) and fuel.

-----------------------------------------------------------------
FLAGS ON THE MAP
-----------------------------------------------------------------
  Yellow in a sector ..... that part of the track turns yellow, banner
                           "YELLOW - S2" and which car is slow
  Full course yellow ..... whole track yellow, banner "FULL COURSE YELLOW"
  (FCY, like a VSC)        (keep to the speed limit, no overtaking)
  Safety car ............. whole track yellow, banner "SAFETY CAR" and a
                           yellow SC box where the safety car is
  Stopped / slow car ..... yellow warning triangle above the car
  Back to green .......... green "GREEN FLAG" banner for 8 seconds

  The banner also says "Pits closed", "Pits open" or "In this lap" (the
  safety car comes in at the end of this lap).
  The voice engineer knows the flag too and won't tell you to push under
  yellow.

  If the banner says "FULL COURSE YELLOW / SC", the game isn't telling the
  dashboard whether it's a safety car. That happens in some online races
  (the server runs the rules), or if UnsubscribedBuffersMask in
  CustomPluginVariables.JSON includes 4. Leave that setting at 0 or 160.

  Sector positions on the map are learned while you drive your first lap.
  Before that the map splits the lap in thirds.

-----------------------------------------------------------------
GOOD TO KNOW
-----------------------------------------------------------------
- The best-lap line and the delta need one clean lap first. Until then the
  delta box says "no ref".
- Lap history starts when you open the page. Refreshing the page clears it.
- Best on a second monitor at 1920x1080. It also fits 1366x768.
- Tire temperatures here come from the same data as the driver dashboard,
  so the "temps don't match LMU" issue in 11 Known issues applies here too.
